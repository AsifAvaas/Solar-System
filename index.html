Controls

  • Drag — orbit the camera
  • Scroll — zoom in/out
  • 0 — focus the Sun
  • 1-8 — focus Mercury..Neptune, in distance order
  • 9 — focus the Satellite
  • Arrow keys — orbit the camera around the Satellite (while focused on it)
  • Left-click — cycle the Satellite's hull texture (while focused on it)
